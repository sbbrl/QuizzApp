# 💕 Valentines Compatibility Quiz

A fun and interactive web-based quiz to help couples and friends discover their compatibility this Valentine's Day! Find out how well you match with your partner, crush, or best friend through a series of engaging questions.

## 🌟 Features

- **Interactive Quiz**: Engaging questions designed to measure compatibility
- **Real-time Results**: Instant compatibility score and personalized feedback
- **Fun & Romantic**: Perfect for Valentine's Day celebrations
- **Shareable Results**: Share your compatibility score with friends and partners
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Works seamlessly on all devices
